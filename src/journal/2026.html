07/29/2026 - 09:33PM:

Hello everyone! It's been a few days since my last post. Recently I've been very hyper-focused, both with Maya and smaller projects, and even optimizing my code, like my website, Vault, and atl (about-this-linux, a simple system fetch).

Maya is becoming increasingly complex and difficult to maintain. A few days ago, the idea of a way to update packages haunted me, because how exactly would that be done? But I managed to implement a good solution that synchronizes recipes with the latest versions and compares them with the installed ones.

In Git recipes, there's a warning saying that there might not be updates, but that it will be done anyway.

All of this is still being implemented and polished, but it's already completely functional!

And another REALLY COOL thing is that I'm finally managing to learn the C language in a "not so painful" way.

I think I got so involved that even terms that are reasonably insurmountable for me because of the "roughness" of the language (like argc, argv[]) were finally truly understood.

Yesterday I studied C quite a bit and, for the first time since studying, I just used my cell phone (for entertainment), and I started thinking specifically about the language and its inner workings: Linux/UNIX system calls, input/output, user interactions, conditionals, formulas, libraries... among many other things.

Which is perfect for me, since C has always been my "dream language".

06/11/2026 - 05:27PM:

Last night was the last straw for me, I officially ended the awl (Arcade.Wrapper-Linux) cycle, its development had been slow for a thousand reasons.

The main reasons are that I developed a hatred for the original project (TeknoParrot), for its creators and for my own project, they have a DLL with an "anti-debug" syscall that acts by "obfuscating" the mapping (which was often done desperately in the dark).

And another reason was because I was going crazy alone, I mapped more than 50 games in about 4 months completely, I didn't have support or help from a single good soul in the community, I'm not going to screw myself over alone in this while the assholes from the original project are neglecting it and still getting paid for it.

I'm tired.

My focus has returned entirely to my studies in C/ASM, and to the free development of Vault, my lightweight mini-launcher CLI for games and Wine programs, AppImages, and Tarballs for Unix-like systems.
